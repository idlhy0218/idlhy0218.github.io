1. Partial demeaning

Each observation is transformed by X*it = Xit − θ X̄i and Y*it = Yit − θ Ȳi. Unit centroids slide fraction θ toward (0, 0) while within-unit slopes remain unchanged.

2. What sets θ

When persistent unit heterogeneity σu² or panel length T grows large, cross-unit levels are noisy relative to within changes, pushing θ → 1. Higher σε² pulls θ → 0.

3. Why θ matters

Because wB ∝ (1 − θ)², θGLS minimizes the standard error when βW = βB, but pulls βRE toward βB (even flipping its sign) whenever unit effects correlate with X.
