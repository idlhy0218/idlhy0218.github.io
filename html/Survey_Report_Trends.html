4. Shift 3: AI Attitudes & Perceived Quality

Key Insight:

Optimism that AI improves work quality plummeted from 20.4% (Fall 25) and 20.2% (Spring 26) down to just 4.0% (Fall 26). Meanwhile, 44.0% of Fall 2026 respondents felt AI lowers work quality.

Across all three cohorts, the vast majority of students (53%–61%) favor explicit guidelines, and 24%–30% want effective prompting instruction. Calls to ignore AI or implement bans remain a minority preference (~8%–18%).

5. Summary of Stable Baselines

  • AI Familiarity: Stable at a mean of 2.9 / 5 across all cohorts (~46%–50% reporting moderate familiarity).
  • AI Usage Frequency for Schoolwork: Remains low at a mean of 2.0–2.2 / 5; over 69% of students in every term report using AI "Rarely" or "Never" for coursework.
  • Paid Subscriptions: Sustained at ~2% to 3.7%, indicating negligible uptake of premium AI subscriptions among undergraduate cohorts.
